Donationware definition
The application is free to use, with all functions available (except optional features), but you are expected to pay your license if you find it useful or if you make any professional use. The donationware model allows selecting the price amount according your means or usage. The webshop is proposing 5 different amounts: The smallest price can be considered as a renewal license price, the default price as the recommended license price, the highest price as a generous support to our R&D team. Thanks for your participation!

Donationware for activation code is following the same principles except that user is periodically invited to donate to get an activation code. This licensing model has been designed as an alternative to SAAS model. Simpler and cheaper for everyone, it is based on simple Challenge / Response code. The Challenge code is pending on your PC, the Response code is delivered by the web-shop after donation. More Info: How our Activation Code works?

Donationware for download is for VB-CABLE A+B and VB-CABLE C+D (additional VB-CABLE). The Webshop will provide you with a personal download link after donation.
Install & Test it First!
One of the advantages of our donationware model is to let you test the application in real situation first! Then you are invited to pay for your license after having validated your workflow and finally found our application useful. Thanks for your participation!

    • Voicemeeter Distribution Rules
    [1] We allow the distribution of Voicemeeter package (Standard or Banana) AS IS together with your application (freeware or commercial software). Voicemeeter may also be embedded in your installer and deployed through a silent installation process, provided that our donationware model remains applicable.

    The donationware model is considered applicable when the End User can clearly identify Voicemeeter as a VB-Audio application and has the opportunity to support the project by making a donation or purchasing a license if they find it useful.

    To comply with this requirement, your documentation, installer, website, or application should clearly mention at least:

  • The origin of Voicemeeter : www.voicemeeter.com.
  • Voicemeeter is a donationware, all participations are welcome.

     [2] If Voicemeeter is distributed in a professional environment (companies, institutions, organizations, public administrations , etc.) where end users or employees are not in a position to identify Voicemeeter independently or contribute to its funding, the distributor must purchase the appropriate number of Voicemeeter licenses directly from our webshop. Licenses may be purchased periodically based on actual deployment figures (for example, on a quarterly basis).

    We also encourage distributors to start with a minimum order of 10 or 100 licenses.

    [3] In the respect of the 2 previous points: You can also use Voicemeeter Remote API to take advantage of all Voicemeeter Audio Services in your own application. Voicemeeter Remote API SDK is free to use for any kind of applications, prototype, POC, educational or commercial projects. More info in Voicemeeter Remote API User/Dev Manual.

    Voicemeeter Potato package cannot be distributed or bundled with other product but can be pre-installed on PCs for free.
